03 · Where bookings leak
Where bookings leak

Great clinics lose patients in the gaps, not in the treatment room.

Your results bring people in. Slow replies, missed calls and no follow-up send them elsewhere, and none of it shows up on a report.

01

After-hours enquiries go cold

Many patients message in the evening, when nobody is at the desk. Whoever replies first usually gets the booking.

02

The phone rings during treatments

Your team can't answer while they're with a patient. The caller rarely leaves a voicemail; they call the next clinic.

03

Empty slots from no-shows

A missed appointment is lost revenue and wasted prep, and chasing it is time nobody has.

04

Interested patients quietly drift

They asked about pricing, then life happened. Without a gentle follow-up, they never come back.
